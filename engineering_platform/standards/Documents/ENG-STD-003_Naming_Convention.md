| Version | v0.1 |

## Prefixes

Platform standards use `ENG-STD-xxx`.

Project-specific Höfrungr artifacts use `HOF-xxx-yyy`.

Examples:

- HOF-SRS-001_System_Requirements_Specification.md
- HOF-HDD-001_High_Level_Design.md
- HOF-ADR-001_Development_Process.md
- HOF-ECP-0001_Engineering_Platform_and_Hoefrungr_Baseline
