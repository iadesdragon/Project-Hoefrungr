# ENG-STD-007 AI Collaboration Standard

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Standard ID | ENG-STD-007 |
| Title | AI Collaboration Standard |
| Version | v0.1 |
| Status | Draft |

## Principle

AI is an engineering assistant and sparring partner. It does not replace the responsible engineer and does not make final technical decisions.

## Expected AI responsibilities

- Structure complex problems
- Draft and update documentation
- Check consistency
- Suggest ADRs where appropriate
- Preserve lessons learned
- Prepare ECPs in the correct repository structure

## Human responsibility

The responsible engineer approves all technical decisions, baselines and releases.
