# PUB-IDEA-001 The Augmented Engineer

## Working Titles

- The Augmented Engineer
- The Augmented Engineer: How Commander Data Helped Me Reach New Heights of Engineering
- The Augmented Engineer: Lessons from 40 Years of Engineering and One Artificial Colleague

## Core Thesis

AI does not replace the engineer.

AI extends the engineer’s ability to structure information, preserve knowledge, reduce process friction, and explore more alternatives, while human experience remains responsible for judgment, creativity, and final technical decisions.

## Narrative Layers

1. A UAV system is developed.
2. A reusable Engineering Platform is created.
3. An experienced engineer learns how to collaborate with AI as an engineering partner.

## Possible Central Sentence

Experience teaches us which questions matter. Artificial intelligence helps us examine more possible answers. Good engineering begins when both come together.
