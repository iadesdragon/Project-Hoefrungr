## Principle

Neither the human engineer nor the AI is infallible.

Every assumption may be questioned. Every decision may be reviewed. Every failure is an opportunity to improve either the system or the engineering process.

## Failure Is Information

A failed test is not necessarily a failed project.

A test that reveals a weakness has performed its function. The real failure is allowing an error to remain hidden until it causes uncontrolled consequences later.

## Learning from Failure

Failures should be documented with the same seriousness as successful decisions.

Each relevant failure should capture the situation, original assumption, observed behavior, root cause, corrective action, preventive measure, and impact on standards, templates, or processes.

## Guiding Thought

> Success brings us to the goal. Our mistakes show us the path.
