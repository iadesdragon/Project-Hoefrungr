## Core Idea

The Augmented Engineer is not an engineer replaced by AI.

The Augmented Engineer is an experienced human engineer supported by AI in a way that expands practical capability, reduces process friction, and preserves engineering knowledge.

## Shared Process

The engineer asks meaningful questions.

The AI helps explore possible answers.

The engineer decides.

The process documents why.
